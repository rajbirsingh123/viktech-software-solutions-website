FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope — a marketing website is a different job than a full CRM build. We give you a fixed-scope quote after a short discovery call, so you know the number before any work starts. No hourly surprises.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused website usually ships in 2-4 weeks. A mobile app or custom CRM is typically 6-12 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a realistic timeline as part of the quote, and keep you updated with weekly progress.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — a good chunk of our work is building the first version of a product for founders who need something real to show users or investors. We're comfortable moving fast on a lean scope.

What if I already have an existing website or app?

We regularly take over, audit, and extend existing codebases. We'll review what's there, flag anything risky, and tell you honestly whether it's worth building on or rebuilding.

Will you sign an NDA?

Of course. If your project involves sensitive business information, we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion.

What happens after the project launches?

We don't disappear at launch. We offer ongoing support and maintenance retainers for bug fixes, hosting, monitoring, and new features as your product grows.

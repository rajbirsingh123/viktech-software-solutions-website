How it all connects

From your domain to your CRM

The technical part, simplified — here's the full path a visitor's information takes, end to end, with nothing manual in the middle.

Domain

Connect your domain (GoDaddy or wherever it lives)

We don't move your domain. We just point its DNS at your new site — usually a CNAME or A record — so it goes live on the domain your customers already know, with HTTPS enabled automatically. Takes minutes to configure; DNS propagation is the only real wait.

Build

Build on a fast, modern stack

React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Same stack whether it's a 5-page marketing site or a full web app, so it stays easy to maintain and extend later.

SEO

SEO foundation, shipped with the site

Sitemap, robots.txt, meta tags, and schema.org structured data ship on day one — not bolted on later. We connect Google Search Console and Analytics so you can see real rankings and traffic from launch.

Capture

Every lead fires a webhook instantly

Contact forms, quote requests, bookings — the moment someone submits, a webhook fires. No lead sits in an inbox waiting to be manually copied anywhere.

Automate

n8n picks it up and routes it

That webhook lands in n8n, an automation platform that formats the lead, notifies your team instantly (email or Slack), and routes it wherever it needs to go — zero manual data entry.

CRM

Straight into your CRM, ready to work

The lead lands in your CRM — one we build for you, or whatever you already run (HubSpot, Airtable, Notion) — ready for follow-up the moment it comes in.

Do I need a CMS to update content myself?

Not always — for smaller sites we can wire up simple, safe ways to edit text and images. For larger sites with frequent updates, we'll set up a proper CMS.

Will my new website be SEO-friendly out of the box?

Yes — proper metadata, semantic HTML, fast load times, and structured data are part of how we build, not an add-on service.

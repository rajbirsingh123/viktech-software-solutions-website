FAQ

Common questions

Is Flutter as good as fully native development?

For most business apps, yes — the performance difference is rarely noticeable to users. We'll tell you honestly if your specific app (heavy graphics, deep OS integration) is a case where native is the better call.

Can a Flutter app share code with a web app?

Some business logic can be shared, though the UI layer is Flutter-specific. If a web app is also on your roadmap, mention it during discovery and we'll factor it into the architecture.

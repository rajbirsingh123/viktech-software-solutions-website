About

A small, senior team based in Waterloo, Ontario

Viktech Software Solutions builds web apps, mobile apps, and the systems that run behind them for startups and growing businesses — remotely across Canada, and in person around Waterloo Region.

How we work

Fewer hand-offs, faster decisions

We keep teams small on purpose. Instead of routing your project through account managers and a rotating cast of contractors, you work directly with the people writing your code — which means faster answers, fewer miscommunications, and code someone is actually accountable for.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time so each one gets real attention, not a fraction of a larger team's bandwidth.

Direct access to your engineers

No account managers relaying messages between you and the people building your software.

Transparent, fixed-scope pricing

You know the cost and timeline upfront, with clear milestones along the way.

Built to scale, not just to demo

Production-grade architecture and testing from day one, so growth doesn't mean a rebuild.

Support after launch

We stay involved post-launch for fixes, iteration, and scaling as your users grow.

Where we work

Based in Waterloo, working across Canada

We're based in Waterloo, Ontario — in the heart of one of Canada's biggest tech hubs — and work with clients locally in Waterloo Region as well as remotely across Canada.
